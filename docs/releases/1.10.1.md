# 1.10.1

## Fixes

- `stim-server` pairs through the `tailscale serve` route on port 7443 by default and refuses to pair while any serve route is exposed through Tailscale Funnel. [#1168](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/pull/1168)
- `stim-server` reports a slow or timed-out device frame capture as delayed instead of failed. [#1170](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/pull/1170)
- The default iOS simulator is a numbered iPhone on the newest runtime that has one, instead of a special model such as iPhone Duo; `ios.deviceType` and `--device-type` are unaffected. [#1174](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/pull/1174)
