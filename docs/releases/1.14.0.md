# 1.14.0

## New

- `@stim-cli/server` pushes attention notifications to paired phones through the Expo push service: a failed build, new log errors, low disk, a stopped app and a slow build. A phone registers an Expo push token with `push.register` and removes it with `push.unregister` or by revoking its pairing. Pushes follow the phone's attention rules: once per occurrence, log errors after the count holds, at most 20 an hour per phone, and more than three at once as one summary. What is already wrong when the server starts or a phone registers does not push. Each push carries the workspace title or Mac name, a short reason and the workspace path, never logs. The Stim phone app, shipped separately, registers for them. [5ff39e2](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/5ff39e2c7867a156d19d4ac62513179e913051ef), [acb19bd](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/acb19bd77e1d0e752182bc97c26084a68df8ec76)
- A Metro stop records its cause. The `supervisor_stopped` log line carries a `stop` field and `status` reports `metro.lastStop`: the Stim command that stopped it (`stim stop`, `worktree remove`, `gc`, a budget reclaim naming the reclaiming workspace, `start --reset-cache`), a signal no Stim command requested, or the dev server's own exit. A supervisor that vanished without recording anything, usually after SIGKILL, a crash or a restart, is logged as `supervisor_vanished` by the next supervisor or `stim stop`. [3e69cf4](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/3e69cf4978ec9a1549508ad43f1d2730db4cb726)
- `stim stop --slot web` closes only the workspace's owned Chrome from `stim web` and keeps its profile, Metro, devices and port. [2fed395](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/2fed39514f139acf82b63a3d68fdaa54a77d2b06)

## Fixes

- Launch verification works in a workspace with named device slots. A Metro bundle delivery now counts for the slot it can be attributed to: an iOS simulator's request is matched to the launched process through `lsof` (`clientPid` on the `bundle_response_*` records), and an Android or physical-device delivery counts while no other slot of the same platform is running. Before, every delivery was ignored once any slot existed, so Android reported `UNVERIFIED` and an iOS slot `bundling` after its bundle loaded. When a running sibling slot makes a delivery unattributable, the launch stays `unverified` and names the device and `stim logs --slot <slot> --source device`. [76f2bd5](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/76f2bd5ca861f2a8b36358b5fbc0d15ff280617f)
- `stim ios` and `stim android` restart an app that is already running, like Xcode's Run, with `simctl terminate` on a simulator and `am force-stop` on an emulator or `--device` phone. A repeat run with an unchanged install no longer ends `launch UNVERIFIED` because the old process kept running and requested no bundle. The launch line reads `restarted running app (was pid N)`. [175fbed](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/175fbeda69cf1bc41a13dcc1356d05e12bc235e1)
- `status --watch --json` exits when its reader is gone, instead of running on, with its watchers and `adb track-devices` child, on a quiet workspace that never wrote again. [6cf7843](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/6cf7843dbbc52586d8efdd1dc272a36d389a631d)
- `stim stop --slot <name>` refuses with `STIM_BAD_ARG` and lists the workspace's slots when it has no such slot, instead of printing `Stopped: port kept` and exiting 0. [2fed395](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/2fed39514f139acf82b63a3d68fdaa54a77d2b06)
- With `web.viewport` at `desktop`, the `stim web` page is 1280x800 as documented. Chrome's own UI had made it 1280x713. [e24bf01](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/e24bf01279c218d9803324d17dd9345ffeba3c64)
- The `--device-type` and `--runtime` mismatch refusals carry their real remedy in `--json`: reap the simulator with `stim worktree remove` or `stim gc --delete`, or pass `--slot <name>` to create the requested one beside it. The remedy said to run `stim doctor`. [d70788f](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/d70788fcd1efa3f8b8d1258786c27382bf742c0b)
- `stim ios --device-type` and `--runtime` refuse with `STIM_BAD_ARG` on a remote run (`--remote` or `ios.remote`), where the remote backend picks its own device, instead of being silently ignored. The `ios.deviceType` and `ios.runtime` settings do not refuse there. [07d41fb](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/07d41fb66885edb0bac614240e128d4b9737201b)
- `stim ports get` and `stim web` no longer print `Port N already in use, trying next...` for each busy port they skip. [67bdb61](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/67bdb61e107c53c3fe0884b73f0570af21a63719)

## Migration notes

- `web` is refused as a device slot name by `ios`, `android`, `device lock|unlock` and `logs`, because `stop --slot web` now means the owned Chrome. A device slot already recorded as `web` still loads, but only plain `stim stop` or `worktree remove` reach it.
- `stim stop --slot <name>` with a slot the workspace has not recorded now fails with `STIM_BAD_ARG` and stops nothing.
- `stim ios` and `stim android` restart an app that is already running, so in-app state from the previous session does not survive a run. If stopping the running app fails, the launch is refused.
- `status --watch --json` exits when the reader of its stdout goes away. On Linux, a detached watch whose stdout is a pipe or socket, such as `stim status --watch --json | cat &`, also exits when the shell that started it exits.
- `stim ios --remote` with `--device-type` or `--runtime` now refuses. Drop the flags for a remote run.
- The `--device-type` mismatch refusal now also offers `--slot <name>`, and the remedy text is on the remedy line instead of in the message.
- While a phone is registered for push, stim-server keeps its `stim status --watch --json` child running with no client connected and reads the disks every minute.
