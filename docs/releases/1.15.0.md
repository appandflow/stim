# 1.15.0

Stim Desktop and the Stim phone app ship through their own workflows; this release covers the six npm packages. Commits that only changed those apps are not listed.

## New

**Builds on another Mac**

- Offload builds to a paired build machine. `stim ios` places simulator Debug builds, `stim android` emulator debug builds and `stim macos` SwiftPM Debug builds on a Mac that has granted this one build access, and installs the returned artifact under the post-mutation cache key. Any failure builds locally. `offload.mode` is `auto` (build here while a build slot is free and the load is under `offload.maxLoadPerCore`, otherwise offload), `force` or `off`. [e433902](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/e43390247d15c954f2ef801d15fb841db122906e), [c5f3514](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/c5f35145b44b8ac059b23dfb73e21e4e9c1d3f97), [4c6542d](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/4c6542dfcf6d05fae2e4961048e0d7b5562f1a27)
- `stim-server` has a `build` capability, granted only on the worker, per client, by approving a build request with `stim-server devices grant <id> --build`. A client names its build machines in `offload.machines`, `doctor --fix` requests access and pins the worker's tailnet node, and the token goes only to that node. A build client never gets `read` or `control`, and every method except `hello` now needs `read`. [eeadd14](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/eeadd14dda37b02ed412f5897640a961d034eb4b)
- Placement is load-aware (`offload.maxLoadPerCore`), reports the machine's capacity in `doctor`, and records where each compiling build ran and why in `status`, build history and `stats`. `offload.machine`, or `--build-machine` on `ios`, `android` and `macos`, names the one machine to require, with no local fallback, or `local` to always build here. A worker holds one of its own build slots for the whole offloaded build, and a client moves to the next machine when one refuses or fails to sync. [d62bc69](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/d62bc696aba74135e91175d220609753f58f777d), [0522c97](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/0522c9745511308b242e6e5202e21e4bd8ccbf4a), [eb41678](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/eb41678923614d54244eab197aa89c6e01e3da08), [fc27bae](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/fc27bae08f084fc86c7e39c11a115b017d93c020)
- An offloaded build survives a dropped connection: the worker keeps the job for 5 minutes and the client reconnects for up to 3 minutes to reattach before building locally. The Gradle daemon stays warm on the worker for `offload.gradleDaemonIdleMinutes` (default 30). `offload.workerRoot` moves the worker's checkouts. [4ad0f8a](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/4ad0f8a16a8961e3796289646103094531cbc37d), [ad307fc](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/ad307fc8c682c1f7f47287cacf67cf36c8ae9223)
- `stim-server service install|status|uninstall` manages a per-user LaunchAgent for stim-server, `service update --release <version>|--from <dir>` and `service rollback` switch builds, and an approved Mac can update the service over the tailnet. On a hosting Mac the service runs under a signed and notarized Stim Host app that owns its macOS permissions; `install` downloads the pinned release, checks its digest and team signature, and installs `~/Applications/Stim Host.app`. [a6a8746](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/a6a8746f50af65ae61e3563c4e16dd6dfa269be0), [8710cf9](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/8710cf93314951af9cf5fd2dc562b2283bbe3e3c), [1bf3791](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/1bf3791b417494b19a9ad55b93a94dcad2a1bef9), [58ae393](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/58ae3939eb33ace4ce36aef8ff927b60ee3fc53c), [387b202](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/387b202624c9afd15f9c9715d47a1207f5e57b41), [28b3566](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/28b35667c371c3fc8f7a59f33d25a2054286373c)

**Native macOS apps**

- `stim macos` builds an owned Swift Package executable in Debug, launches an isolated development bundle signed ad hoc, and shows its window in Stim Desktop and the phone app. `macos.product` and `macos.infoPlist` select the product and development plist, `macos.arguments` passes launch arguments, and `macos.resources` and `macos.assetCatalog` stage resources and a compiled asset catalog before signing. It refuses with `STIM_BAD_ARG` when `macos.product` is unset. [038a8ed](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/038a8ed9c4fc3e9d9435d6f90ae2a7c56b5b75de), [3fd1587](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/3fd158738e0362c2919a9902471ce364f99aed67), [9f9b4d0](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/9f9b4d0d297bf793a5733c8dcccc4b917a83bbcd), [1bf6e18](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/1bf6e18d676f924779e6617b0d337e9e78e930d4)
- `stim macos --remote <machine>` runs the app on an approved hosting Mac named in `hosting.machines`, streams and controls it from Desktop and the phone, copies its logs to the client for `stim logs`, and reports a session the host stopped in `stim status`. Viewing and Control follow the app's front window or a pinned window. An offloaded macOS build is handed to the hosted session on the same Mac. [9a2b135](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/9a2b1355c7940d781182ef21ad3fbddb6dfb1d72), [3db30ee](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/3db30ee92e77d406f436c2089bbd061021e0aaad), [316ee42](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/316ee42a87dabdf52a9acca4c86c717b43526ef2), [8341d3d](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/8341d3d5b3a63336612eb631ac8013a547a45d73), [422a3a1](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/422a3a163fec7ca66b496a65685b8e18c88eac5e), [3c2ff56](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/3c2ff56d85ae88f1b1c4e18fc2c335fcf9df8c0f), [6e4e82c](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/6e4e82c363e61a5572f4563337c82985e5f5e34f)
- Agents drive a hosted macOS app through agent-device `macos-app` leases; the adapter starts only when the daemon advertises that backend, and `STIM_AGENT_DEVICE_BIN` names the agent-device binary. [38da60b](https://github.com/appandflow/stim/commit/38da60b7d50546b4a8b40142408654f41c6988bd)

## Migration notes

- stim-server pushes changed. The failed-build, log-error, low-disk, stopped-app and slow-build pushes of 1.14.0 are replaced by `started`, `stuck`, `looping`, `finished`, `machine` and `control` notifications, and every category starts Silent, so a paired phone gets no alert until it opts in.
- A `stim-server` client that holds only `build` can no longer read status: every method except `hello` needs `read`, and a build client never gets `read` or `control`.
- `stim` and `stim-server` exit 1 with `STIM_NODE_UNSUPPORTED` on a Node below 22.12.0. Run them under a supported Node, for example with `nvm exec`.
- A single-arch iOS simulator build is cached under a key that ends in its arch, in the CLI and in `@stim-cli/expo-build-cache`. The first iOS simulator build after upgrading misses an entry stored by 1.14.0.
- A parked simulator or emulator keeps its apps, and adoption removes other third-party apps and clears the adopting app's data. Before, parking erased the device.
